3. Safe default: 500x500 px is ideal for most modern phones (supports high-density screens and potential zoom-in animations).

4. Resizing/compression: Resize and compress images client-side before uploading. This saves user bandwidth and reduces backend storage.

5. Target file size: 100 KB – 300 KB per image (JPEG, 80% quality). This gives a good balance between clarity and fast load times.
